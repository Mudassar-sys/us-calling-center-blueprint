Yes, a European company can own and use US numbers

The question that decides everything else is not where you are registered. It is whether the carrier has verified who you are.

US carriers sell local and toll-free numbers to companies registered outside the US. The carrier verifies your company with its own registration and business address; a US address is not part of it. New carrier accounts can start on tight limits for numbers and outbound calls until that verification passes, so it is the first thing we start.

Every outbound call is signed with an attestation level that the receiving network reads before the phone rings:

AThe carrier knows your company and the number is yours. This is what your calls get once your company is verified and the numbers sit in your own account. BThe carrier knows you but not your right to the number shown. CThe call entered the US from another network and nobody can vouch for it. Calls that start abroad almost always land here, and they get answered less.

That is why the dialer and the carrier connection sit in the US while your agents work wherever they are.

The FCC has open proposals on showing verified caller names and on marking calls that originate abroad. We build on verified identity and verified numbers now, and adjust the setup when final rules are published.

What the carrier checks

  • Company name, registration number and business address
  • An authorized representative of the company
  • Identity checks on the company before high-volume calling opens
  • The numbers you will show, assigned to your verified company profile

What we do with your numbers

  • Prepare the verification pack and open every account in your company's name
  • Register each number with the free caller registry that feeds carrier spam analytics
  • Show local numbers that match the lead's area
  • Cap calls per number per day and rotate across the pool
  • Check labels every week and replace a flagged number before it costs you answers

The setup, end to end

Three places: where your agents sit, what runs in the US, and who is on the other end of the call.

Your agents

Nothing to install beyond a browser and a headset. Audio is encrypted from the agent's screen to the US server, and the phone call itself is placed from that server, not from abroad. Supervisors can listen, coach in the agent's ear or take over.

In the US

One cloud server runs the dialer for ten agents with room to grow, behind an edge that only accepts traffic from the carrier. Leads are checked before they reach the dialer, never after.

Your data

Your company is the controller under GDPR, so every vendor signs a processing agreement, offshore access runs under transfer safeguards, agents see only what the call needs, and every playback of a recording is logged.

It gives you predictive dialing, ratio dialing for power and progressive pacing, preview and manual dialing, plus inbound and blended calls in one system, with no per-seat licence. Your leads, recordings and transfer logic stay on a server your company owns. The cost is administration, which our setup and managed operations cover.

Why not Aircall or JustCall

Both are good business phone systems. Aircall offers a power dialer and no predictive dialing. JustCall keeps predictive dialing in its separate sales dialer plans, not in its standard plans, where Pro is $49 a user a month on annual billing. For a lead floor that needs predictive pacing and tight drop-rate control, either one is a workaround.

    • A new lead is dialed within 60 seconds when its calling window is open and an agent is free
    • A lead on an opt-out or Do Not Call list is never dialed, and the reason is logged
    • A lead outside its calling window waits for the next window
    • The same lead arriving twice creates no second call and no second transfer
    • A test call from each of your numbers reaches a verification line that reads the signature and shows full attestation
    • On a live transfer the agent introduces the caller, the buyer accepts, the agent leaves and the call continues
    • When a buyer does not answer, the transfer is logged as failed, the agent keeps the caller and nothing is billable

We have also run our own offshore lead-generation and customer-service floor of 60 to 70 people, and our engineers work US hours every day for clients across US time zones.

    2. Which dialer would you recommend, and why?

      VICIdial on a US cloud server. It covers predictive dialing, ratio dialing for power and progressive pacing, preview and manual dialing, plus inbound and blended calls in one system, carries no per-seat licence, and keeps your lead data and transfer logic under your control. The trade-off is administration, which our setup and managed operations cover. If you would rather own no servers, Convoso is the managed alternative at a per-seat price.

    3. Which providers can issue US numbers to a European company without a US address?

      Several large US carriers onboard companies registered abroad. They verify your company with its own registration, address and an authorized representative, assign the numbers to that verified profile, and sign your calls with full attestation once both are approved. Where they differ is how they treat dialer traffic: fees on unanswered attempts, calls-per-second limits and rules on very short or unanswered calls. In the blueprint you get those terms in writing from the carriers that fit your traffic, and you choose.

    4. Can you handle the complete setup?

      Blueprint in 3 working days. Two agents live 7 working days after the carrier approves your company and we have access. Ten agents 3 working days after that. Carrier verification runs in parallel with our build.
